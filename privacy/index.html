Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Visuals of BF is a property photography and videography business run by Brandon Fernandes, a sole trader based in Uddingston, Scotland. We are the data controller for the personal information described here. Contact us at info@visualsofbf.com or 07432 518831.

What we collect and why

When you book or enquire

Through our online booking form, contact forms, email, phone or chat, we collect:

  • your name, email address and phone number, and for estate agents, your agency;
  • the property address, shoot date and time, access arrangements and any notes you give us;
  • for agent bookings, the homeowner's name, phone number and email, so we can arrange access and send preparation guidance;
  • how you found us, for example the link or website that brought you to the booking page. This does not use cookies.

We use this to quote, schedule and carry out the shoot, deliver photos, video and floor plans, invoice you and answer your questions. Our lawful basis is taking steps to enter into, or performing, a contract with you. For homeowner details passed to us by an agent, it is our legitimate interest in carrying out the shoot the agent has booked.

When you visit our website

We use cookies and similar technologies only with your consent, which you give through the cookie banner:

  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd), to understand how visitors use the site;
  • Meta Pixel and Conversions API (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd), to measure and improve our Facebook and Instagram advertising;
  • essential cookies set by our website platform, Wix.com, which the site needs in order to work.

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie settings link on the site.

Photos and video

We photograph and film properties, not people. We avoid capturing people, personal documents and identifying details, and we will remove anything identifying on request. With the agent's or owner's permission, we may use images in our portfolio and on our social media.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We use trusted providers to run the business, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Wix.com: website hosting, forms and chat;
  • Google: Workspace email, Calendar, Drive and Sheets (where bookings are stored and files delivered), Analytics and YouTube;
  • Meta: advertising measurement, with your consent;
  • Dropbox and Pic-Time: delivering finished photos and video;
  • Slack: internal booking notifications;
  • Instantly: email outreach to estate agents;
  • our editor and our accountant, where needed for the work or for tax.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on approved safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

YouTube

Some property videos are uploaded to YouTube on behalf of our clients using the YouTube API Services. By viewing or using that content you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke our access to any YouTube account you have connected at Google security settings.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and invoicing records for six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as HMRC requires. Enquiries that don't become bookings are kept for 12 months. We keep delivered photos and video in our archive so we can re-supply them if needed. Ask us and we will delete them.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it or object to its use. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email info@visualsofbf.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we update this policy, the date at the top will change.
